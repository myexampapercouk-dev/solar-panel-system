Our story

Built by people who wire, weld and climb roofs.

Solar Panel System started with one crew and a promise to explain every rupee of a quote. Today the same crew still shows up on site — we've just gotten better at it.

How we got here

2017

Two engineers, one van

Founded in a two-room office, installing 3 kW rooftop systems for neighbours and friends of friends.

2019

First commercial rollout

Fitted a 40 kW array on a textile unit — our first project big enough to need its own crew lead.

2021

In-house design team

Brought layout design and grid paperwork in-house so quotes stopped taking two weeks to turn around.

2023

Battery storage added

Started pairing panels with battery banks so customers keep power through evening cuts, not just daylight.

Today

3,000+ systems and counting

A dedicated maintenance team now checks in on every system we've ever installed, every single year.
