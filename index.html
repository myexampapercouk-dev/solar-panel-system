Questions

How long does installation take?

Most home systems go in within one to two days once the design is approved.

Do panels work on cloudy days?

Yes, at lower output. Panels still make power under cloud cover, and a battery can store the extra.

Will I still pay an electricity bill?

Most homes still pay small fixed charges, but the power you generate cuts most of the usage charges.

What warranty do you offer?

Panels carry a 25-year performance warranty, and our workmanship is covered for five years.
