What we do

Four ways we put the sun to work.

From a single rooftop to a full commercial array, every service below is handled by our own crew — no subcontracted installs, no handoffs.

Rooftop systems for homes

Panels sized to your roof and your bill. We handle the layout, the grid connection paperwork and the net-metering application.

Solar for businesses

Larger arrays for shops, schools and factories, with flexible financing and priority maintenance response times.

Battery storage

Store the power you make during the day so it's there for the evening — and for the next scheduled power cut.

Care and maintenance

Yearly cleaning, panel inspection and remote output monitoring, so a dip in performance gets caught early.

Ground-mounted arrays

For farms and open plots — steel-framed arrays angled for peak output, sized from 10 kW to 100 kW+.

Net-metering & subsidy paperwork

We file the DISCOM application and central/state subsidy paperwork on your behalf, start to finish.

How any project runs

  1. Free site survey

    We visit, measure your roof and check for shade.

  2. Custom design

    You get a layout, a written quote and a savings estimate.

  3. Installation

    Our crew fits the system and handles the grid paperwork.

  4. Switch on

    We test the system and show you how to track output.

Questions about our services

Which service is right for my home?

Most homes start with a rooftop system sized to their average bill; a battery is worth adding if power cuts are frequent in your area.

Do you handle the subsidy paperwork?

Yes — our team files the net-metering and applicable subsidy applications for you as part of every installation.

Can you service a system another company installed?

In most cases yes. Send us the panel and inverter make and model through the contact page and we'll confirm.

How often does maintenance happen?

Our standard plan includes one full inspection and clean per year, with remote monitoring running continuously.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us about your home or business and we'll recommend the right system size — no obligation.
